IWZ custom lobby video decoder

This folder contains the dynamically loaded FFmpeg libraries. Ship all five
DLLs and FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt with IWZ-MOD's data files. The game still starts
without them; the custom video menu reports that the decoder is unavailable.
