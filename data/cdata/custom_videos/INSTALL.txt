CUSTOM LOBBY VIDEOS

Put your videos in this folder. Open BARRACKS in the Zombies pre-game lobby,
then MOVIE SCREEN beneath LOBBY MUSIC. REFRESH and select a video. Choose
ORIGINAL MOVIE to return to the usual lobby film. Your choice is saved for
the next session.

Supported containers: MP4, M4V, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, WMV, MPEG/MPG, TS, M2TS.
Common codecs include H.264, HEVC, VP8, VP9, AV1, MPEG-2, MPEG-4 and WMV.
Encrypted or damaged videos cannot be played. Files may contain audio, but
only the video plays; the lobby music is unchanged.

Videos loop on the theater screen. Their proportions are preserved with
black bars where needed. Up to 4K input is supported and scaled to fit the
screen. Playback is local to each player and stops when leaving the lobby.
